Strategy rules

Each closed bar at 3/5/15/60-min is classified from futures price × OI change: long buildup / short covering (bullish, +1), short buildup / long unwinding (bearish, -1). Decision = sum of the latest score per timeframe: ≥+3 STRONG LONG, +1..+2 LONG, 0 NEUTRAL, mirror short. Algo window 08:59–15:40 IST, data refreshes every 1 min. Paper trades: STRONG LONG buys 1-OTM Call, STRONG SHORT buys 1-OTM Put (1 lot, nearest weekly expiry); exit on score flip against or 15:25 square-off. No real orders.
